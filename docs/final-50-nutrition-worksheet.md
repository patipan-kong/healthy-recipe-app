# Final-50 correction nutrition worksheet

These are approximate ingredient-based estimates per serving, not laboratory measurements or brand-specific label claims. Only recipes whose composition changed materially or whose estimates were flagged were recalculated. Two servings per batch; all listed oil, sauce, bread, rice and garnish are included. Water adds no energy. Draining, trimming and cooking losses are not modeled; thus sauce/stock sodium may be conservative. The small galangal/lemongrass infusion is counted even though the tough pieces are removed.

Method: for every ingredient, multiply the generic per-100-g profile below by the assumed edible batch weight / 100; sum the batch and divide by 2. Round macros, fiber and kcal to whole units; sodium to 10 mg. Profiles are transparent generic estimation assumptions, not newly retrieved USDA records. Actual products vary, especially tofu firmness, coconut-milk dilution, bread weight, paste and stock sodium. Light coconut milk assumes 75 kcal/100 ml, tofu 100 kcal/100 g, stock 100 mg sodium/100 ml, reduced-sodium soy 3333 mg sodium/100 g; consult actual labels during final nutrition review. A bread slice is assumed 32 g, cooked brown rice cup 195 g, cooked jasmine rice cup 158 g, cooked lentil cup 198 g and cooked chickpea cup 164 g. Falafel dry chickpeas are weighed before soaking. All values are already labeled estimates by the application.
