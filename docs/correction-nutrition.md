# Bounded correction nutrition ledger

Date: 2026-09-13. All values are estimates, not laboratory measurements or individual dietary guidance. Only the 18 recipes below were fully recalculated. No composition was inflated to preserve previous numbers.

## Basis and limitations

Each listed recipe makes two servings. For each nutrient, sum (ingredient weight × profile / 100), divide by two, then round once to whole kcal, g or mg. All measured oil, sauce, dressing and pickle liquid is counted as consumed. Water contributes zero. No unlisted oil, salt or cooking-water salt is assumed. Meat weights are raw edible portions; canned tuna/beans are drained; pasta and glass/rice noodles are dry; rice, quinoa and lentils are already cooked.

Generic profiles below are explicit working assumptions consistent with common food-composition values, not a claim that every entry was individually retrieved or analytically verified. Brand, tofu firmness, draining/rinsing, vegetable size and cup packing materially affect results. They are recorded in full to permit independent replacement/recalculation. The firm-tofu assumption is calcium-set, protein-dense firm tofu (17.3 g protein/100 g); softer local tofu will produce lower protein.

The soy profile uses [Kikkoman less-sodium soy sauce](https://kikkomanusa.com/products/less-sodium-soy-sauce/less-sodium-soy-sauce-15-oz/): 590 mg sodium per 15 ml, with a 10 kcal, 2 g protein, 1 g carbohydrate working label basis. [Kikkoman less-sodium teriyaki](https://kikkomanusa.com/products/less-sodium-teriyaki-marinade-sauce/) confirms 310 mg sodium per 15 ml; 15 kcal/1 g protein/3 g carbohydrate is the working macro assumption. These are example products, not required brands.

Vegetarian hard cheese uses an explicit generic 390 kcal/28 g protein/31 g fat/700 mg sodium per 100 g assumption, not a verified brand label. A [non-animal-rennet Parmesan-style product exists](https://www.belgioioso.com/products/parmesan/); the recipe requires vegetarian certification and microbial rennet. Check the purchased label rather than substituting ordinary Parmesan. Low-sodium stock is modeled as 50 mg sodium/100 ml; rinsed beans as 200 mg/100 g. These are product-sensitive estimates, not guarantees.

Cup assumptions: oats 80 g; cooked brown rice 195 g; jasmine rice 158 g; sushi rice 186 g; cooked quinoa 185 g; cooked lentils 198 g; chickpeas 164 g; white beans 179 g; black beans 172 g. Each large egg is 50 g edible portion. Small onion 70 g, small cucumber 158 g, medium carrot 61 g, pepper 119 g, zucchini 196 g, medium tortilla 45 g, half avocado 75 g. Two spring onions 30 g, garlic clove 3 g, nori sheet 2.5 g, lettuce leaf 10 g. Oil teaspoon 4.5 g; sugar teaspoon 4 g; sesame teaspoon 3 g; miso tablespoon 17 g; peanuts tablespoon 9 g; yogurt tablespoon 15 g. Other herb/spice conversions are explicit in the ingredient ledger.

Liquid quantities in the ledger are ml for soy, teriyaki, fish sauce, coconut milk, vinegar, citrus juice and stock; their profiles are per 100 ml. Other quantities are grams.
